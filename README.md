# Below the Deep

**The yacht is gone. The night is long. Something under the water has not finished with you.**

*Below the Deep* is an underwater survival horror game that runs entirely in your browser: one HTML file, no install, no account. Put on a Meta Quest, open the page on a PC, or tap it on your phone. Same world, same ocean, same thing circling beneath your lifeboat.

You and your friends were on a summer holiday. Then the hull scraped, a shadow longer than the boat slid past in the dark, and the sea took everything but one small lifeboat. Now it is just you, a handful of scrap, and a very big ocean.

## What you will do

* **Survive on a lifeboat.** It is your base. Build oars, a motor, a canopy, a lantern, a stove and an anchor. Fish from the deck. Cook what you catch.
* **Dive.** Swim with real arm strokes in VR. Breathe from a tank that runs out. Watch the water go from sunlit blue to black as you sink, and keep an eye on your oxygen.
* **Explore.** Wrecks, a freighter, coral reefs, kelp, hot vents, ice fields, three islands and caves. Your map fills in only where you have actually been.
* **Be hunted.** Reef sharks. An anglerfish that waits for you to switch your light on. Eels in the wrecks. And the leviathan, which hunts by sound. Every ping, every engine, every panicked splash carries.
* **Survive the sea itself.** Storms, lightning, tornadoes and whirlpools, each with four stages from mild to extreme. A barometer warns you. A whirlpool will swallow your boat and spit it out far away.
* **Send the signal.** Find the parts, build the beacon, switch it on, and stay alive long enough for a ship to find you. Who is still on the boat when it arrives decides how the story ends.

## Play it your way

|||
|-|-|
|**VR (Meta Quest)**|Full room-scale VR in the browser. Arm-stroke swimming, a forearm status display, a comfort vignette and an on-screen console keyboard.|
|**PC**|Mouse and keyboard, or a gamepad. Every key can be remapped.|
|**Phone**|Touch controls with a visible joystick.|
|**Multiplayer**|1 to 4 players. One of you hosts and shares a short room code, the others join with it. Proximity voice chat, revive downed friends, tag things for each other. If the host leaves, someone else takes over.|
|**Modes**|Survival, Hardcore (no respawns, harsher sea, your save is deleted when you die) and Creative (no threats, flight, and a console for spawning anything).|

## A word of warning

It gets dark. It gets loud. It gets very quiet, and that is worse.
